# Manhunter — Commercial Model

Status: V0 COMMERCIAL DIRECTION
Date: 2026-10-08

## Strategy
Manhunter is commercial-ready from the foundation, but monetization is phased.

## Layer Monetization
Intelligence: subscription, credits, source packs, API.
Execution: execution credits, hosted usage, BYOK plan, premium templates.
Distribution: workflow subscription, team features, follow-up automation.
Full loop: premium, team/agency, enterprise, API.

## Initial Principle
Do not optimize for pricing complexity before proving:
1. demand quality
2. opportunity quality
3. demo quality
4. conversation rate
5. deal rate

Monetize the highest-value bottleneck after evidence.

## Commercial Readiness
V0: workspace boundary, usage hooks, provider abstraction, BYOK boundary, feature flags and deployment abstraction.
Later: authentication hardening, billing, subscriptions, payments, self-serve onboarding, quotas, teams and API keys.

## Spin-Off Rule
A layer may become an independent product only after independent demand, repeatable value, distinct distribution, sustainable economics and a clear product boundary are demonstrated.

Until then, keep the layers unified as one revenue loop.
