# ORDVELA INTELLIGENCE — Opportunity Scoring Model
Status: V0 CANONICAL

## Goal
Rank opportunities by commercial usefulness, not raw engagement or volume.

## Dimensions
Demand strength, intent strength, urgency, budget signal, problem specificity, fit, reachability and evidence confidence — each 0–100.

## Suggested Weight
20% demand + 20% intent + 15% urgency + 15% budget + 10% fit + 10% reachability + 10% confidence.

Weights must be versioned and configurable.

## Explainability
Expose component scores, weighting version, evidence used and calculation timestamp.

## Learning
Evaluate scores against replies, qualified conversations, proposals and revenue. Never silently rewrite historical scores.
