Sources, evidence normalization, demand extraction, intent, scoring, ranked feed and evidence view.

Blueprints, constrained execution, BYOK, prototype generation, deployment and demo URLs.

Target selection, message generation, human review, follow-up, outcomes and conversion analytics.

Auth, workspaces, usage/credits, BYOK onboarding, hosted provider, billing, plans, self-serve and API boundary.

Only after evidence: more sources, advanced scoring, teams, agency workflows and APIs.
