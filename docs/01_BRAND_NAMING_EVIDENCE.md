# ORDVELA — Naming Evidence

## Source

Batch 7 controlled-invention naming research.

## Construction

The research describes ORDVELA as a fused construction using `Ord-` and `-vela` with documented linguistic inspiration rather than a literal phrase.

- `Ord-`: associated in the research with Old English/Old Norse `ord` and Latin `ordo`, carrying ideas around point, origin, beginning, and order.
- `-vela`: associated with Latin/Iberian vocabulary around veil/sail and related movement/unveiling concepts.

The intended brand meaning is **an abstract structure that organizes and reveals**, rather than a literal translation.

## Known Linguistic Caveat

`vela` is a dictionary word in Spanish/Portuguese contexts. This does not by itself establish a conflict, but it reduces linguistic distinctiveness and should remain part of trademark and market review.

## Naming Principles Preserved

- avoid ordinary dictionary words as master brands
- avoid direct phonetic cousins of rejected names
- avoid generic startup suffixes
- use controlled invention rather than random fantasy naming
- prioritize collision safety and ownability over aesthetics
- retain an Indonesia-first pronunciation and spelling test
