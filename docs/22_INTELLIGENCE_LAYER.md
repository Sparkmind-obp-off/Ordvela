# ORDVELA INTELLIGENCE — Intelligence Layer
Status: LOCKED FOR EXECUTION

## Objective
Find public evidence of genuine demand that is actionable and commercially relevant.

## Pipeline
COLLECT → DEDUPLICATE → NORMALIZE → EXTRACT DEMAND → VERIFY EVIDENCE → CLASSIFY INTENT → ESTIMATE COMMERCIAL SIGNAL → SCORE → RANK

## Initial Sources
Web search, Reddit, jobs/freelance demand, Threads, X, Instagram and Facebook.

## Evidence
Store source, stable reference, author/account when available, timestamp, original text/snippet, context, problem, desired outcome, urgency, intent, budget signal, geography and confidence.

## Strong Signals
Explicit need, active request, specific problem, time pressure, budget evidence, consequence of inaction and willingness to evaluate a solution.

## Output
An Opportunity Candidate, not merely a lead.

## Operator UI
Ranked feed showing score, problem, evidence, source, commercial signal, recommended action and original-source access.
