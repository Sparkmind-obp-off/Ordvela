# ORDVELA Brand Architecture

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** LOCKED FOR EXECUTION  
**Master Brand:** ORDVELA

## 1. Architecture Model

ORDVELA uses a **master-brand-led / branded-house** architecture. ORDVELA remains the canonical identity; functional families explain what each part of the ecosystem does.

```text
ORDVELA
├── Ordvela Systems       -> software and business systems
├── Ordvela Intelligence  -> AI, research, discovery, decision systems
├── Ordvela Studio        -> implementation, services, design, delivery
├── Ordvela Labs          -> experiments, R&D, prototypes
├── Ordvela Platform      -> RESERVED: genuine multi-product platform
├── Ordvela API           -> RESERVED: reusable external API product
└── Ordvela Marketplace   -> RESERVED: functioning marketplace/network
```

## 2. Master Brand: ORDVELA

ORDVELA is the parent identity for the technology, AI, software, automation, business-system, and digital-operator ecosystem.

Rules:
- ORDVELA is the canonical master brand.
- Formal documents use **ORDVELA**; product/UI contexts may use **Ordvela**.
- New normal products should inherit the ORDVELA family rather than create unrelated master brands.
- Domains, social identities, repositories, documentation, and future legal assets should be consolidated where practical.

## 3. Primary Families

### Ordvela Systems

**Role:** production software and operational business systems.

Use for workflow systems, CRM/ERP-like products, vertical systems, operator software, and production applications.

Example:
`Ordvela Systems — Demand Operator`

### Ordvela Intelligence

**Role:** intelligence and decision layer.

Use for demand intelligence, opportunity discovery, research engines, scoring, AI analysis, market signals, and decision support.

Example:
`Ordvela Intelligence — Demand Discovery`

### Ordvela Studio

**Role:** commercial implementation and delivery.

Use for client implementation, automation, custom development, UI/UX delivery, technical services, and consulting.

Example:
`Ordvela Studio — AI Workflow Integration`

### Ordvela Labs

**Role:** experiments and R&D.

Use for prototypes, technical validation, emerging concepts, and ideas not yet ready for production. A validated Labs project may graduate into Systems, Intelligence, or another production family.

## 4. Reserved Families

### Ordvela Platform — RESERVED

Activate only when multiple products, capabilities, accounts, data layers, or integrations genuinely operate as one platform. A dashboard alone is not a platform.

### Ordvela API — RESERVED

Activate only when ORDVELA exposes a reusable API/infrastructure product intended for external developers or integrations. Do not use it merely as the name of an internal API.

### Ordvela Marketplace — RESERVED

Activate only when a functioning marketplace/network connects supply and demand.

## 5. Product Naming Grammar

Default hierarchy:

```text
ORDVELA
└── Family
    └── Product / System
        └── Module / Feature
```

Example:

```text
Ordvela Systems
└── Demand Operator
    ├── Discovery
    ├── Opportunity Database
    ├── Scoring
    └── Action
```

The product name describes the product's job. The family identifies its business context.

## 6. Naming Rules

1. ORDVELA remains the master identity.
2. Prefer `Ordvela + functional descriptor` over unrelated invented brands.
3. A product may have an internal codename, but public naming follows this architecture.
4. Family names describe durable business functions, not temporary marketing language.
5. Reserved families cannot be presented as active businesses until their activation criteria are met.
6. One product has one canonical public name.
7. Avoid unnecessary descriptor stacking such as `Ordvela Systems Platform Engine Suite`.
8. New family proposals require an architectural reason, not merely a naming preference.
9. Legacy brands should be migrated deliberately rather than duplicated indefinitely.

## 8. Business-Model Alignment

The architecture maps directly to the operating model:

```text
Demand Intelligence
        -> Opportunity Database
        -> Scoring
        -> Action
        -> Revenue / Delivery
```

- **Ordvela Intelligence** discovers and interprets demand.
- **Ordvela Systems** stores, orchestrates, and executes workflows.
- **Ordvela Studio** converts capabilities into client delivery and revenue.
- **Ordvela Labs** tests new opportunities before production investment.

## 9. Legacy Migration Principle

Existing assets such as SparkMind and other project identities should not automatically remain parallel master brands.

- Production software -> **Ordvela Systems**
- Intelligence/research -> **Ordvela Intelligence**
- Services -> **Ordvela Studio**
- Experiments -> **Ordvela Labs**
- No active business role -> archive

Migration should preserve useful technical assets, repositories, domains, and customer continuity where practical.

## 10. New-Family Decision Gate

Before creating a new public family, answer:

1. Does the offer have a materially different business role?
2. Does it require an independent audience or market identity?
3. Would staying under ORDVELA create genuine confusion?
4. Is the distinction durable rather than temporary?
5. Is there an operational reason to own a separate identity?

If mostly **no**, keep it under ORDVELA with a functional descriptor.

## 11. Core Principle

> **One master brand. Few meaningful families. Clear product names. No unnecessary brand fragmentation.**

ORDVELA should operate as one coherent technology/business ecosystem, not as a collection of unrelated startups.
