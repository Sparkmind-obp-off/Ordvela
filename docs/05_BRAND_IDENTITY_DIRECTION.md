# ORDVELA — Brand Identity Direction

## Identity Objective

Create an identity that feels like a serious technology systems company without defaulting to generic neon-AI aesthetics.

## Visual Direction

Working direction:

- restrained
- geometric
- structural
- high legibility
- premium but not ornamental
- technical but approachable
- strong typography
- modular visual system

## Logo Direction

Explore a wordmark-first identity. A symbol may be developed only if it adds recognition or system utility.

Potential visual concepts:

- ordered modular geometry
- controlled convergence
- an abstract point/origin motif
- structured pathways
- subtle `O` / `V` geometry

Avoid literal icons for AI, robots, circuits, brains, rockets, or generic infinity symbols unless a strong original treatment emerges.

## Typography

Prioritize a contemporary sans-serif system with:

- strong lowercase/uppercase legibility
- good UI rendering
- broad Latin support
- clear numerals
- professional enterprise use

Specific font selection is deferred until identity exploration.

## Color Direction

Start from a restrained neutral foundation and test one distinctive accent. Do not lock a final palette until the identity system is explored across light/dark UI, documents, websites, and product surfaces.

## Verbal Identity

Use short, direct statements. Prefer concrete capability over hype.

Example:

> Systems that make complexity usable.

This is exploration copy, not a final tagline.

## Identity Gate

Visual identity work should begin after the naming verification gate reaches a sufficiently clear state. No expensive external identity commitments should be made while ownership remains materially uncertain.
