# ORDVELA INTELLIGENCE — Commercial Model
Status: V0 CANONICAL

## Principle
Commercial readiness belongs in the architecture; monetization follows evidence.

## Revenue Surfaces
Intelligence: subscription, credits, source packs, API.
Execution: execution credits, managed usage, BYOK plan, templates.
Distribution: workflow subscription, team features, follow-up automation.
Full loop: premium/team/agency/enterprise/API.

## First Proof
Demand quality, opportunity quality, demo quality, conversation rate, deal rate and revenue per opportunity.

## V0 Hooks
Workspace boundaries, usage accounting, provider abstraction, BYOK boundary, feature flags and deployment abstraction.

Do not build complex billing before product value is proven.
